Our story

The Tembea story

Tembea was founded with a simple dream: to help travellers discover the beauty, diversity and adventure of Africa. The word "Tembea" means "to travel" or "to walk around" in Swahili. Inspired by the spirit of exploration, we created a travel company that connects people with remarkable destinations and authentic experiences.

Our journeys bring together unforgettable wildlife encounters, beautiful coastal escapes and opportunities to experience local cultures. From the sweeping plains of the Maasai Mara to the turquoise waters of Zanzibar, we believe every journey should create meaningful memories while respecting the places and communities we visit.

"Travel is more than visiting new places. It is about connecting with people, discovering different cultures and leaving every destination better than we found it."

— sumaya halane, fictional founder of Tembea

At Tembea, responsible tourism is at the heart of everything we do. We aim to support local communities, encourage wildlife conservation and help travellers explore Africa with curiosity, respect and appreciation.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

What destinations does Tembea offer?

We feature six destinations across Kenya and Tanzania, including Maasai Mara, Serengeti, Amboseli, Diani Beach, Zanzibar and Watamu.

When is the best time to go on safari?

June to October is generally a popular dry-season period for many East African safari destinations, although wildlife experiences vary throughout the year.

Can I customise my travel package?

Yes. You can contact us with your preferred destination, travel dates, budget and travel style to discuss a suitable itinerary.

Does Tembea promote responsible tourism?

Yes. We encourage wildlife conservation, respect for local cultures and environmentally responsible travel practices.
