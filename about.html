About Tembea

Our Story

Tembea was created from a passion for travel and a love for the beauty of Kenya. We believe that travelling is one of the best ways to discover different cultures, meet new people and create unforgettable memories.

Our goal is to make discovering Kenya easier by connecting travellers with exciting destinations, unique experiences and helpful travel information. Whether you want a wildlife safari, a relaxing beach holiday or a cultural adventure, Tembea helps you explore your options.

"My dream was to create a platform that helps everyone experience the beauty of Kenya. Every journey should tell a story worth remembering."

— sumaya halane, Founder of Tembea

Today, Tembea continues to celebrate Kenyan tourism by encouraging responsible travel, supporting local communities and helping visitors discover the country's remarkable attractions.

Our Mission

To inspire people to explore Kenya through accessible travel information, memorable experiences and responsible tourism.

Our Team

Our fictional team brings together travel enthusiasts, destination researchers and customer support specialists who share a passion for showcasing Kenya.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What destinations can I explore with Tembea?

You can explore safari destinations such as Maasai Mara and Amboseli, as well as coastal destinations such as Diani, Lamu and Watamu.

How do I plan a trip with Tembea?

Visit our destinations page, choose a destination and submit an inquiry through our contact form.

Does Tembea offer budget-friendly travel options?

Yes. We aim to help travellers discover options for different budgets, from affordable trips to luxury experiences.

When is the best time to visit Kenya?

The best time depends on your destination and activities. Wildlife viewing is particularly popular during the dry seasons, while coastal destinations offer different experiences throughout the year.
