our story

tembea was created to help travellers discover beautiful scenerios adventures and view across Eat africa and beyond. our goal is to make travelling easier and fun for our customers.

we believe travelling helps people to see different cultures and food across the globe. travelling with us is a good choice to make people enjoy travelling and help them understand the different cultures

we created Tembea to inspire people to travel and enjoy the adventures, scenerios, views, culture and food
