Explore Our Destinations

Discover unforgettable experiences across Kenya, from exciting wildlife adventures to peaceful coastal escapes.

Safari Destinations

Maasai Mara

Famous for its wildlife, big cats and the annual Great Migration, Maasai Mara is one of Kenya's most popular safari destinations.

Best time to visit: July to October.

Amboseli National Park

Amboseli is well known for its large elephant herds and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Best time to visit: June to October.

Lake Naivasha

Enjoy boat rides, bird watching and beautiful scenery around this freshwater lake in the Rift Valley.

Best time to visit: June to October.

Coastal Destinations

Diani Beach

Diani offers beautiful beaches, water sports, coral reefs and relaxing seaside accommodation.

Best time to visit: January to March.

Lamu Island

Explore historic Swahili architecture, traditional dhow boats and the rich cultural heritage of Lamu.

Best time to visit: January to March.

Watamu Beach

Watamu is known for its marine national park, beautiful beaches, snorkelling and coral reefs.

Best time to visit: January to March.
